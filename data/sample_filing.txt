ITEM 1. BUSINESS

Nimbus Cloud Systems, Inc. is a provider of cloud-based infrastructure
monitoring and observability software for mid-market and enterprise customers.
The Company sells primarily through an annual subscription model and serves
over 4,000 customers across North America and Europe. Revenue grew strongly
during the fiscal year driven by new customer acquisition and expansion within
the existing base, with net revenue retention remaining a key strength of the
business model.

ITEM 1A. RISK FACTORS

The Company operates in a highly competitive market and faces competition from
both established platform vendors and emerging point solutions. A significant
portion of revenue is concentrated among a limited number of large customers,
and the loss of one or more such customers could adversely affect results.
The Company is also exposed to regulatory uncertainty regarding data privacy
across the jurisdictions in which it operates. Rising cloud infrastructure
costs have placed pressure on gross margin, and the Company may not be able to
pass these costs through to customers in a timely manner.

MANAGEMENT'S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS

Revenue for the fiscal year increased to record levels, reflecting continued
demand for the Company's observability platform. Management notes that gross
margin compressed modestly during the year as a result of higher cloud hosting
and input costs, partially offset by pricing actions taken in the second half.
Operating leverage improved as sales and marketing spend grew more slowly than
revenue. The Company maintains a conservative balance sheet with modest
borrowings under its revolving credit facility and a strong cash position,
providing flexibility to invest through the cycle.

OUTLOOK

Looking forward, management expects continued revenue growth, though at a
moderating rate as the business scales. The Company intends to prioritize
investment in product and selective acquisitions while maintaining
disciplined cost management. Forward-looking statements are subject to risks
and uncertainties that could cause actual results to differ materially.
